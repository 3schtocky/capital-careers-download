# Capital Careers

A desktop app with your own AI career agent. Pick your industry, fill in your profile, and the agent searches the job boards you use, fills out internship applications for you, and stops for your OK before anything is submitted.

## Download

Go to **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** and download:

- **Windows:** `Capital-Careers.exe`
- **Mac (Apple silicon):** `Capital-Careers.dmg`

## You need

- A Claude Pro or Max plan. The agent thinks with your own subscription; applying uses a lot of it, so Max is smoother
- Google Chrome
- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), or a Mac with Apple silicon on macOS 12 or newer

## Install

**Windows:** save `Capital-Careers.exe` somewhere you'll keep it (for example Documents) and double-click it. The first time, Windows SmartScreen may say it protected your PC because the app isn't signed: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. It takes a few seconds to start.

**Mac:** open `Capital-Careers.dmg` and drag Capital Careers onto Applications. The first time, right-click the app, choose **Open**, then **Open** again (it isn't notarized).

## First run

1. Pick your industry: Finance, Engineering, Actuarial, or type your own
2. Connect your Claude subscription: **Install** sets up Claude Code with Claude's official installer, then **Connect** opens your browser to log in to Claude
3. Follow the arrow to **Profile** and fill in what roles you're looking for, your school and your resume
4. In the Browser tab press **Start browser** and sign in to Handshake, LinkedIn or Indeed once
5. Say hi to your agent in the chat on the right

Everything stays on your computer. The agent never submits an application without your click, never signs in for you and never sends messages for you.
